# Oira front-end: design direction (READ before building any UI)

**Hard rule: Oira's product UI must not read like a typical crypto/DeFi app**: dark, technical, metallic. Oira must read **soft, ladylike, beauty**, from the first pixel: palette, type, spacing, motion, copy, iconography.

## Before writing UI
Gather proper beauty-design references first (do not wing it):
- Study the calibre of modern beauty brands (Glossier, Rhode, Ilia, Merit, Saie, Kosas, Refy, Fenty, Rare) for restraint, softness, and generous whitespace.
- Establish a **fresh Oira design system**: soft/warm palette (blush, cream, warm neutrals, deep-tone-inclusive accents, never "lightening"), a refined type pairing (an elegant serif or humanist sans, soft weights), gentle motion, rounded and airy layouts, human and tender copy.
- Deep-skin-first still holds: imagery, swatches and defaults must center rich/deep tones.

## Constraints carried over
- Human, convincing copy; NO em-dashes / needless hyphens; no AI tells.
- Full functionality parity wherever it ships (web + eventual Seeker/Android).
- The whitepaper's design system (INK/PLUM/AURORA/TEAL/GOLD/COPPER/CREAM in `DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`) is for the *paper*, not necessarily the app, the app palette should be softer/warmer; reconcile when the app design system is made.
