House rules
  1. Still surfaces → Silk Edge; moving surfaces → Passing Light.
  2. Below 48px → mono marks only. The rainbow never appears at favicon scale.
  3. The wordmark never enters a circle (avatars are the mark alone).
  4. One ribbon per composition: iris on night, melanin on daylight; never both.
  5. Clear space around the mark = the height of the O's counter.
  6. Gold appears only when something is sealed or attested.
  7. The gradient never sits behind text.
  8. The rainbow is the light on the subject, never the subject.

Taglines, daylight: "your colours, kept." · night: "sleep. your palette stays awake."
