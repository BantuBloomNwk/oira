How these numbers are counted

Every woman counted here agreed to take part, took five scans of her skin on her own phone, saw exactly what would be sent, and chose to send it. A reading is one of those scans. If she takes part again on the same phone, her new readings are added but she is not counted twice. On a second phone she would be, because we keep nothing that could tell us it is her.

We never collect a name, a photo, an email or an address, and the picture never leaves her phone. Countries with fewer than three women are grouped as "other" so nobody can be singled out. Anyone can ask for their readings to be deleted, so totals can go down as well as up.

For a technical check, the same totals are served as data at /api/study/count. Questions: info@myoira.com.
