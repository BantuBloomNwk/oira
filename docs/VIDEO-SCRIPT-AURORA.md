# Oira demo video: Aurora's script

This is the video we submit. It is the **app**, not a terminal, with Aurora's voiceover. Warm and
human, no jargon. Nobody says "enclave", "TEE", "on-chain", or "commitment" out loud. The goal is
that a person who knows nothing about crypto watches it and wants it.

Target length: 60 to 75 seconds. Record the app screen (the phone flow in `app/`), Aurora reads the
lines over it. One optional 5-second cutaway at the end for the people who want proof.

A separate, purely technical "prove it yourself" walkthrough lives in the public repo as
`docs/VERIFY.md`. That one is for developers and judges who want to run it. It is not this.

---

## Before recording

- Have the app open and seeded so the offer board shows a real pending offer (post one from the
  Brand Studio first, e.g. a foundation offer, so "a brand is asking tonight" is true on screen).
- Do the seal once beforehand so her palette is real and the overnight resolves cleanly.
- Keep it to her phone. No code, no terminal, no wallet addresses in frame.

## The script

*(Reworded 2026-07-12 for a more natural, spoken feel: same beats, same length, easier to say out
loud and easier for a non-crypto audience to follow on first listen. Checked against everything we
shipped since the last pass, and nothing here changes, because none of it belongs in this video.
Seeker/wallet features are opt-in extras for the people who already want them, not part of the story
we're telling someone meeting Oira for the first time. That split is intentional; see the note at the
end of this file.)*

**1. The hook (0:00 to 0:10)**
On screen: the opening card, then her face in the scan frame.
Aurora: "I've spent my whole life helping people find the colour that's actually theirs. The problem
was never the colour. It was everything you had to hand over just to find out."

**2. Her reading, sealed (0:10 to 0:22)**
On screen: the scan completes, the reading appears (season, undertone, depth), then the seal
animation.
Aurora: "Oira reads your colour right there on your phone. Your season, your undertone, your depth.
Then it seals it, so that reading belongs to you, and only you. Your photo never leaves your hand."

**3. She sleeps, her agent works (0:22 to 0:34)**
On screen: the night screen, her phone dark, the quiet "answering privately" state.
Aurora: "While you sleep, a brand can ask one question. Not who you are, just does she match what
we're looking for. Your agent answers for you, quietly, and you don't lift a finger."

**4. She wakes up paid (0:34 to 0:48)**
On screen: the morning board, the real matched offer with the brand name and the amount, the "paid
privately" line.
Aurora: "You wake up, and a brand that wanted exactly your shade has already paid you for it. The
only thing they ever found out was yes. Not your face. Not your reading. Not your wallet. Just yes."

**5. Her rules (0:48 to 0:58)**
On screen: the night-rules screen, the payout floor and per-brand limit, an offer being skipped.
Aurora: "You set the rules. What a question is worth. How often a brand gets to ask. Who you'd
rather never hear from at all. Anything outside that gets turned away, and nothing gets shared."

**6. The close (0:58 to 1:08)**
On screen: the "is your privacy real" proof screen, then the Oira mark and myoira.com.
Aurora: "And you can check, any time, that this is real, not just something we're telling you.
Your most private information, finally working for you, without ever becoming the product. This is
Oira. Find your colours at myoira.com."

**Optional proof cutaway (1:08 to 1:15), no voiceover**
On screen: cut to the public record for her match. A slow scroll showing there's no shade, no answer,
no wallet, just sealed values. A caption: "This is everything the public record shows."

## Notes for Aurora

- Speak the way you would to a friend who asked what you have been building. The lines above are a
  guide, not a cage. If a phrase feels stiff in your mouth, change it.
- The strongest beat is 4, waking up paid with only a yes shared. Slow down there.
- Never claim it is finished everywhere or that money is guaranteed. "A brand paid you" is true and
  enough. We do not oversell.
- If we want a second language cut, the same script works in translation; the app already speaks
  several, and more Global South languages are coming through our own voice pipeline.
