Accuracy study

Runs the real scan pipeline, not a copy of it, so what it measures is what the app does. Read docs/ACCURACY-STUDY.md before using it: the order of operations is what keeps the data from being noise. Instrument reading first, on the same spot, seconds apart. Nothing here leaves this phone.

Three readings make one trial. The app's, taken by the camera when you tap Capture. The instrument's, typed in from the device. And the Monk step, which you assign by eye from a printed Monk scale, because the app's own Monk answer is one of the things being graded and cannot also be the answer key.

The trial still records, and the summary still gives you a repeatability figure, which is how far the app's own readings sit from each other on the same spot. That half of the question needs no instrument. Accuracy, which is how far it sits from the truth, does.
