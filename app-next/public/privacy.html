What I do with what I know about you

Last updated 2 September 2026

Most privacy policies are written so that nobody finishes them. This one is short because there isn't much to say, and there isn't much because Oira was built so that there would not be.

Your face never leaves your phone

When you let me read you, the camera runs in the app on your own device. The picture is never uploaded, never stored and never sent anywhere. I look at it, I work out your colours from it, and the picture is gone the moment the reading is done.

What I keep is the reading, not the photograph. Numbers about colour and light. There is no image of you anywhere in Oira, on my side or yours, because there is nowhere in the app that an image is written down.

What is kept, and where

All of this lives in the app on your phone and nowhere else:

  • Your reading. Colour measurements, how confident I was, and what the light was doing.
  • Your season, your palette and anything you corrected me on.
  • Your rituals and whatever you told me you have been using.
  • The key that locks all of it to you.

If you photograph a product you own so I can tell you how it really looks, I keep the colour I measured and throw the photograph away. That measurement is about your camera, not about you, and it stays on your phone too.

How you are locked to it

Your fingerprint, or your face, whichever your phone uses. From that your phone gives me a secret only it can produce, and I turn that into a key. I never see the secret and I never hold the key. There's no password to remember and nothing to write down, and because your phone syncs its own fingerprint keys, a new phone gives you the same key back without you doing anything.

If you already have a Solana wallet you can use that instead. Then the key stays inside your wallet and I only ever ask it to sign something, which it will ask you to approve.

What goes on Solana, and what does not

When you seal a reading, one number goes onto the Solana network. That number is made from your reading the way a fingerprint is made from a finger: it cannot be turned back into the thing it came from. Not by me, not by anyone.

Your measurements do not go on there. Your face does not go on there. Your name does not go on there. When a brand asks whether one of their shades suits you, they get a yes or a no, and that is genuinely all they get.

The part I cannot undo. Solana is a public ledger, so once a seal is written nobody can unwrite it, me included. It stays a number that was never your face and can't be turned back into you, but it does stay. Worth knowing before you seal rather than after.

What leaves your phone, and only when you ask

  • When you ask me a question and I cannot answer it on your phone, the question goes to my own server so a bigger model can answer it. Your season and your palette go with it. Your photograph, your measurements and your key never do.
  • When you seal, the sealing goes through my server to reach Solana. It carries the sealed number and your signature. It never sees your key.

Nothing else is sent, ever. There's no analytics in Oira, no advertising, no tracking pixel and no third party watching you use it. I don't sell anything about you, and there's no arrangement under which I could start: the data is on your phone, not mine.

Deleting all of it

Open the menu and tap Delete everything. Your colours, your readings, your rituals and your key are removed from your phone straight away. There's no form to fill in and nobody to email, because there's nothing on a server for me to go and delete.

Two things survive that, and I'd rather say so. Any seal already written to Solana stays, as above. And your fingerprint key is kept by your phone rather than by me, so if you want that gone as well, remove the Oira passkey in your phone's own settings.

Children

Oira isn't built for children and I don't want data from them. If you're a parent and you think your child has used it, delete everything from the menu and it's gone from that phone.

If this changes

If I ever change what happens to your information, this page changes first and the date at the top changes with it. If a change ever meant something new leaving your phone, you would be asked, not told.
