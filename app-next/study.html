Accuracy study

Runs the real scan pipeline, not a copy of it, so what it measures is what the app does. Follow the study protocol from the research team before using it: the order of operations is what keeps the data from being noise. Instrument reading first, on the same spot, seconds apart. Nothing here leaves this phone.

Three readings make one trial. The app's, taken by the camera when you tap Capture. The instrument's, typed in from the device. And the Monk step, which you assign by eye from a printed Monk scale, because the app's own Monk answer is one of the things being graded and cannot also be the answer key.

The circles are the patches read on her face; they should sit on skin. For paper in frame, hold a plain white sheet flat in the "paper here" box, tilted slightly away from the window so it is not glaring. If it says the paper is overexposed, set exposure to -1, then -2 (Android only; an iPhone ignores it).
